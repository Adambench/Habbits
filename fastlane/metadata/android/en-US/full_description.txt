Habbits is an offline-first habit logger built around the run of the day: Before
Fajr, Fajr, Shuruq, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and anything that fits anywhere.

Prayer times are computed on the device from your location and calculation
method, so there is nothing to download and no timetable to keep up to date. The
window that is live right now is marked, and the day opens on it.

Habits can be simple checks or measured amounts with their own units and step
sizes, scheduled every day, on chosen weekdays, or every few days.

Devices sync through an append-only log in any folder you already replicate —
Syncthing, Nextcloud, a git checkout, a USB stick. There is no account, no
server and no quota, and each device only ever writes its own file, so there is
nothing to conflict.

The app requests no Android permissions. It never reaches the network.
