Reminders that actually arrive, and stats that respect sleeping habits.

Reminders now use exact alarms, so a phone left idle on a desk or nightstand no
longer holds them back for hours. Each alarm knows which window it is for, so
Anytime and Before Fajr reminders, and ones set to go off before a window opens,
now fire. Switching reminders on arms the six prayer windows straight away. They
re-arm after an app update or a time zone change, and settings now show when the
next one is due, what is blocking them if anything, and a button to send a test.

Stats now know when a habit was asleep. A habit counts only on the days it was
awake: one asleep today still gets credit for the months it was done, and one
woken yesterday is not marked missed for the weeks it slept. A habit added
mid-year is no longer counted as missed every day before it existed. Past days
in the day view show what was active then.

Your data carries over untouched.
