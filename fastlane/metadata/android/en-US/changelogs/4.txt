Swipe between days, a clearer Habits screen, and reminders that actually arrive.

Swipe left or right on the day view to move between days. The header now has
labelled Stats and Habits buttons, and the day arrows sit beside the week strip.

The Habits screen explains itself: each habit says when it repeats and what it
tracks, and a menu names every action. Archived habits have their own section,
and deleting asks first and offers to archive instead. The editor is grouped
into clear sections, reads the habit back in a sentence as you edit, and asks
before throwing away unsaved changes.

Reminders now use exact alarms, so an idle phone no longer holds them back for
hours. Anytime and Before Fajr reminders, and ones set before a window opens,
now fire. They re-arm after an update or a time zone change, and settings show
when the next one is due, what is blocking them, and a button to send a test.

Stats now know when a habit was asleep: it counts only on the days it was
awake, and a habit added mid-year is not missed for the days before it existed.
Past days in the day view show what was active then.

Your data carries over untouched.
