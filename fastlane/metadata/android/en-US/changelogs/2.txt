Stats.

A completion heatmap, streaks, per-habit rates, and breakdowns by time of day
and day of the week. Reach them from the ◔ button in the day header.

Rates count only the days a habit was actually due, so a Friday-only habit is
never marked missed on a Tuesday. Sleeping and archived habits are left out of
the rates and reported separately rather than dragging every number down.

This update changes no data: your habits and history carry over untouched.
