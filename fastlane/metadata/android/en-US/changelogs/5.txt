Move a night prayer across midnight, history and all.

A habit in Before Fajr now has "Move to Isha…" in its menu, and a habit in
Isha has "Move to Before Fajr…". Moving to Isha dates every day you logged it
one day earlier, so a night's prayer counts on the evening it began rather than
the morning after; moving back dates them one day later. Each move exactly
undoes the other, and it syncs to your other devices like any other change.
