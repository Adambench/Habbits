Reminders and per-habit stats.

Reminders are anchored to the prayer windows, so an offset like "Maghrib + 20"
follows the sun through the year instead of being a clock time that drifts. They
stay quiet when everything in the window is already logged, and they are off by
default.

Tap any habit in the stats list for its own history: gaps, how often a miss is
followed by a recovery, and a plain reading of the pattern behind the numbers.

Habits that share a name now show their prayer window beside it.

Your data carries over untouched.
